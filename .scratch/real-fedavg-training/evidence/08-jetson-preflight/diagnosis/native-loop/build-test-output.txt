## Added Periodic Lines

`RX_BATCH` is emitted by the existing `Aggregator::dump_stats()` cadence:

```text
<ts_ms>\tRX_BATCH\t<batches>:<packets>:<elapsed_us>:<max_elapsed_us>:<max_packets>
```

`GRANT_SEQ` is emitted by the existing client `log_send_ts` cadence:

```text
<ts_ms>\tGRANT_SEQ\t<accepted_count>:<last_accepted_sequence>
```

The sequence is a server-global grant sequence. Gaps are expected when other
nodes receive grants and are not a packet-loss estimate.

`CLIENT_LOOP` is emitted by the same existing client cadence:

```text
<ts_ms>\tCLIENT_LOOP\tpending_unauthorized_fec_due=<count> zero_timeout_polls=<count>
```

No existing line was removed. No per-frame printf was added. No authorization,
FEC flush, scheduler, READY, poll timeout, or packet dispatch behavior was
changed.
